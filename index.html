Explore. Learn. Innovate.

The College of Computer Studies (CCS) is dedicated to developing competent, ethical, and service-oriented professionals in the field of computing. Guided by Lasallian values, we strive to provide quality education, relevant skills, and meaningful experiences that prepare our students for the challenges of a digital world.
